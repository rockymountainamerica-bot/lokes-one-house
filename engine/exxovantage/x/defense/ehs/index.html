How ExxoLift works

  • What it is: A passive, upper-body exosuit.[source]
  • How it works: A compact, flexible frame with a dual spring system that transfers load off the back.[source]
  • Load path: Passive and spring-loaded. Part of the upper-body load moves from the upper trunk, through the back strap, to the thighs.[source]
  • Designed for: Lifting, bending, and handling material and tools. Exxovantage describes it as reducing the load on the back, shoulders and arms during those tasks.[source]
  • Strain and fatigue: Exxovantage says its exosuits are designed to minimise strain and fatigue in labour-intensive tasks.[source]
